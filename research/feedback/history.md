# Earlier feedback retained as research requirements

Source: Ana's project feedback from 13–14 October 2026. App-specific bugs and unrelated personal context stay outside this research record.

| ID | Visible concern | Research consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H-001 | Morphs appeared nearly instantaneous. | Judge intermediate shapes; retain a visible default transition. |
| H-002 | Press should flatten a rounded body; release should rebound. | Couple physical shape and lensing through one evolving geometry. |
| H-003 | Increased depth looked like linear zoom. | Compare central magnification against curved peripheral compression. |
| H-004 | Surfaces appeared artificially sanded flat. | Separate surface shape, normal-derived response and painted shading. |
| H-005 | Dragging against a boundary should anchor one side and stretch the other. | Share geometry across shape, source sampling and interaction. |
| H-006 | Some filters missed the background gradient or showed no lensing. | Include the completed background scene; verify nonzero source displacement. |
| H-007 | Sliders showed both refracted and unrefracted track lines. | Audit compositing and silhouette replacement separately from ray geometry. |
| H-008 | Some controls disappeared without a subtle light/dark outline. | Study reference-supported boundary light; do not substitute a uniform painted border. |

These are visible requirements, not measurements of Apple's thickness, refractive index, pressure law or light field. Later explicit geometry-only and stage-isolation instructions govern implementation.
