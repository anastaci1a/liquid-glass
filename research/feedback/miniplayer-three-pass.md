# Narrow bright rim and opposing gaps

This feedback follows study100, particularly its third and fourth passes. Raw wording is retained locally outside Git. The scope is three full passes within four hours, without dispersion.

| Region | Updated requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Opposing cap diagonals | Outline disappears completely at upper-right on the right cap and lower-left on the left cap; smooth transitions surround each gap. |
| Peak width | Start near one third of the current maximum-strength plateau; do not conflate plateau width with inward fade width. |
| Outline color | Increase peak brightness substantially while retaining yellow and other colors; test a visibly oversaturated contrast and then a middle ground. |
| Shoulder shape | Reduce top/bottom squashing further toward the earlier shape, keeping the corrected tip placement. |
| Lens interior | Measure fixed color patches before changing saturation; distinguish body color from outline color. |
| Shadow | Mostly satisfactory; retain as a baseline and assess in every pass. |
| Family priority | Prefer the expanded outline and clearer references; transfer exactly the same model to compact and disclose mismatches. |

Brightness, saturation and the renderer's physical meaning remain hypotheses. The description of additive light motivates a display-response test; it does not identify calibrated radiance or permit clipping to hide failures.
