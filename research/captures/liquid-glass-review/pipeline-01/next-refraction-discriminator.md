For an axisymmetric prescribed front p(r)=(r,f(r)), shared Snell yields the actual internal direction v(r). A putative back hit q(r)=p(r)+t(r)v(r), and a desired source-plane feature u(r), define the outgoing direction w(r) from q toward(u,-D). Snell demands its normal be parallel to n_i*v-n_o*w (with correct orientation/valid branch). Tangency gives [n_i*v-n_o*w] dot [p'+t*v'+t'*v]=0, an ODE for the physical intersection distance t, not an image-coordinate warp. Every computed point/normal must be globally integrable into one actual back, positive-thickness/closed, correctly oriented and free of invalid/re-entering paths.
