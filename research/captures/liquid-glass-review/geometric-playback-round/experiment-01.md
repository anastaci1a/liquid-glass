# G01 — Flat front, convex back, orthographic camera

- Visible defect: the play lens has a too-abrupt shoulder and a reversed outer-edge strip absent from Ana's reading of Apple. References: frozen 219/190 target, registered219/180 photograph; historical accepted play pass03 remains immutable/filtered/empirical. Local references are the pass04/06 rim crops and Ana's feedback in pass-04/feedback.md.
- Hypothesis: a flat front with a flat central back and progressively curved convex back bevel can refract strongly without a radial fold. This is an explicit bounded solid, not a displacement correction. Orthographic camera is the first test, not a verdict on a finite-distance camera.
- Coordinates: L=245px is circle radius; x right, y down, +z towards camera. Front z=0. Back z=-h(r), r<=1. Side r=1, -edge<=z<=0. Background z=-D. Camera orthographic along -z.
- Height: h=edge+depth*(1-u^p), u=max((r-core)/(1-core),0), with edge>=0, depth>0, p>=3 and 0<=core<1. The plateau and bevel meet C2. h'<=0/h''<=0 certify a concave height/convex back. Its exact gradient defines the exit normal. All cross-sections and rays use this same function.
- First proposals: core.6/depth.02/p3 and core.3/depth.04/p3, n1.5, D2, edge0. Third proposal only if a target-independent nonfold depth bound justifies it; no image fitting. Camera/background/thickness are geometric quantities.
- Shared core: Snell vectors, path status, flat-front orthographic tracing of certified concave height surfaces. Entrance is normal incidence; back intersection is exact at z=-h(x,y). Transmitted outward ray cannot reenter a concave back because z(t)+h(x(t)) is concave with negative initial derivative. Slope bound guarantees no TIR; reject any actual violation, background clearance failure or upward exit. TIR/missing paths are not converted to background samples. Finite-camera/side-entry is not implemented or claimed by this restricted tracer.
- Frozen: input PNG bytes, registration, target pose, native masks/crops, canonical triangle coverage. Source and output optical blur ZERO. Historical filtered baseline stays separate. Geometry render has neutral transmission and no new outline, shadow, lighting or grading fit. Freeze historical foreground color separately for the diagnostic so icon work cannot disguise geometry. Report this nuisance difference from the historical full material.
- Observations: surface/gradient/Snell tests; radial map and fold locations; same-surface sections/rays; enlarged target/historical/candidate crops and whole-control image errors. Broad RGB differences alone do not prove a geometric fix. AA is finite pixel integration, not optical blur. No final animation on an unpromising model.
- Budget: 20 minutes after implementation starts; at most3 substantive surface proposals,24 small geometry evaluations total,3 native diagnostic renders,0 image optimizer. Intermediate checkpoint after geometry invariants and before image rendering. Stop on invalid path/domain, budget, or after3 proposals; review hypothesis then. Implementation/setup time reported separately; do not reset this budget.
- Classification: mathematical correctness, visible fidelity, cross-control transfer and device speed assessed separately. A negative result is useful if it resolves the stated shape/camera question. No score-only acceptance.
