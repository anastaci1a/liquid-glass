# Curved front / flat back comparator

Private proposal, not an accepted pass or recovered Apple surface. It tests the alternative location of refracting curvature after the flat-front family underfit. No source/shape/mask changes.

- Orthographic camera along −z; top z=h(x,y), bottom z=0, backdrop z=−gr. h/r=(1−T)[t+bx(x/r)²+by(y/r)²], same crowned unequal-axis footprint T≤1. h=0 on the boundary, h>0 inside. This permits a central depression/thicker ring or a dome depending on coefficients; no prescribed Apple shape claim.
- The actual height gradient defines outward front normal N=(−hx,−hy,1)/sqrt(1+|grad h|²). D=(0,0,−1), eta=1/n, a=−D·N. Transmitted internal ray V=eta D+(eta a−sqrt(1−eta²(1−a²)))N.
- Bottom intersection P1=(x,y)+h Vxy/(−Vz). At the flat glass/air exit, Wxy=nVxy, Wz=−sqrt(1−|Wxy|²). F=P1+gr Wxy/(−Wz). Invalid branch/side hits are rejected, not clipped.
- Global slope bound M=2 max(1,|qprime(a)|)Pmax+2 hypot(|bx|half_x/r,|by|half_y/r). Internal tilt δ=atan(M)−asin(sin(atan(M))/n). Require M tanδ<1 and n sinδ<1. The first guarantees the ray descends faster than the top can fall. Since top meets bottom at its boundary, the ray cannot cross a side above bottom. Convex footprint and checked bottom point also keep the internal segment inside. The second guarantees transmitted bottom rays. Below z=0 there is no glass to reenter.
- Fixed n1.5, initial box t[.001,.12], bx/by[0,.12], g[.5,25], declared before map/native fitting. This is a geometry comparison, not wavelength-resolved transport. Inherited reflection, dispersion proxy and tone remain empirical material layers and are not evidence of physical fidelity.
- Invariants: analytic height gradient, unit propagation vectors, independent tangential Snell equations at BOTH interfaces, zero-index-contrast identity, mirror/axis-rotation/scale, conservative slope bound, sampled internal top clearance and per-ray bottom footprint. The inherited crown's equal-axis case is not circular; no circle-limit claim.
- Surface integrability is not equivalent to zero curl of an arbitrary finite-gap output map. [Bösel and Gross](https://arxiv.org/html/1510.01129) derive the coupled surface/ray requirement and distinguish a small-angle approximation. This comparator avoids independently assigned normals by differentiating one explicit height.
