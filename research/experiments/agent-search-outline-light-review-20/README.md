## Geometric and scene scope

The original flat-front circular height is radially nonincreasing and concave. The proposed ordering argument uses bounded height and slope:

$$
0\le h\le H,\qquad 0\le -h_r\le M<1,\qquad
\delta\le\frac{2HM}{1-M^2}<R.
$$

The displacement bound keeps the first reflected front-plane intersection within the disk. Convex occupancy supplies event ordering. This does not certify transmission, supported scene radiance, an entire sampled image or a different curved-front surface.

The proposed emitter has polar support from 30 to 80 degrees, ten-degree shoulders and two fixed radiances. Its direction points outward toward the scene. Separate s/p products must be retained; no cosine or inverse-square multiplier is applied to infinite-distance radiance.

The sixteen saved samples include first returns inside and outside that proposed support. Sparse angular samples do not establish a complete native field. Actual future directions, membership and critical/TIR status require their own whole-field check.
