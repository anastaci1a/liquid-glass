## Shape and optical consistency

The new body is an ellipse dilated by a disk. Inner ellipse axes are the existing half-extents minus the disk radius. It preserves all four axis extrema, has smooth positive curvature and slightly bowed long sides. Only major length differs between compact and expanded. The disk radius is not an unchanged local cap-curvature radius.

Distance to the closed inner ellipse is convex and one-Lipschitz. Its actual gradient drives both unchanged radial height profiles. There is no independently adjusted normal or image warp. For exterior points, the closest-point multiplier satisfies:

$$
\frac{a^2x^2}{(a^2+\lambda)^2}+\frac{b^2y^2}{(b^2+\lambda)^2}=1,
\qquad
\boldsymbol e=\left(\frac{a^2x}{a^2+\lambda},\frac{b^2y}{b^2+\lambda}\right).
$$

The actual front/back gradients, mirror, scale, circle, unit-index, exact-boundary and near-edge fixtures pass. Both native grids complete. Every original guard and the whole body pass finite Gaussian-plus-bilinear source support before RGB. Incident blur remains before refraction.

The original sufficient slope and monotone escape conditions are re-established for the certified convex-core distance. Deep inside the ellipse, the edge-distance helper is truncated; it is not claimed to be an exact deep-interior signed distance.

## Separate display effects

Shadow darkens only supported clear AA subrays, with a shared four-pixel softness and two-pixel downward offset. Every lens sample and fully covered completed pixel remains exact. It is neither refracted nor included in the optical surface.

The inner highlight brightens the existing linear-light color and gently desaturates it. It is composited after the original foreground chain. Yellow remains a brighter yellow instead of becoming a pure white overlay. Angular weakening and the 2.6-pixel fade are shared hypotheses, not identified Apple shader parameters.

The final development combination uses shadow strength `0.1` and the lowest sampled highlight training loss. All three bounded scalar searches reached their evaluation limit near the upper bound; no converged optimum is claimed. No extra coefficient or geometry setting followed.

## Own verification and visible limits

Four original starting images and score dictionaries replay exactly. Twenty-eight material outputs replay through separate algebra within `5.961e-8` absolute RGB. Stored metrics and guard booleans replay exactly, with no acceptance tolerance. All sixteen expanded and twenty-two compact original partial pixels remain scored.

Disk and anisotropic parallel-ellipse area controls pass independently. Two positive integration orders agree within `7.735e-8` opacity. This finite-order agreement does not certify a global optical integration error or the physical correctness of the proposed response.

All thirty-two full comparison sheets and the four final fixed-crop sheets were inspected. Compact rim visibility improves, especially against colored backing. Expanded A's upper-left and lower edge become too bright. Incorrect cap returns and upper lettering remain. The overlay cannot repair those underlying source-feature errors.

Conditional source mosaics, hypothetical scene radiance and unassigned later reflected power remain limitations. Smooth shape validity and implementation consistency do not establish a uniquely recovered Apple model.
