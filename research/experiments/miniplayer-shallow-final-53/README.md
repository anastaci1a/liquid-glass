## Numerical and source limits

Sampling49 reproduces cached AA2 exactly, yet several AA4-to-AA8 reflected-signal differences remain large. Thin-line preview brightness cannot be treated as converged. This is camera integration, not optical blur. Increasing samples is not a surface improvement and cannot silently replace the original score protocol.

Finite panels are hypothetical scene illumination, not recovered reference lighting. Later reflected power remains unassigned. Source support is conditional temporal-mosaic availability, not hidden photographic truth. Input validity, geometric consistency and visible fidelity remain distinct claims.

Foreground and dispersion receive explicit assessments. Unknown effects are not called absent. The shadow component changes only clear underlay; its fully covered pixels replay exactly. A complete continuous-boundary verdict still requires a converged common integrator.
