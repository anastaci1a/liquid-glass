## Source roles stay separate

Direct front reflection toward the backdrop requires its raw four-texel support. A backdrop path that passes through glass requires the full radius 21 incident-kernel and bilinear support. The audit loads four original known masks, constructs their finite support and checks each relevant endpoint. No RGB is sampled.

Upward or lateral exits need environment radiance that the frozen cases do not observe. Unsupported downward endpoints are not clamped, assigned white or omitted. Conditional source-mask availability is not hidden photographic truth. Source support and ray power do not supply an emitted radiance value.

The model therefore remains unsuitable for a complete reconstruction from these inputs. Its actual grazing normals demonstrate a potential optical role, but the broad source-feature mismatch and rim fidelity remain unresolved. Further image work needs justified scene support and a bounded treatment of internal transport, not a painted contour or stronger arbitrary lamp.
