# Structured research passes

A full pass is one bounded cycle through isolated modeling stages. It may conclude with an accepted component, useful negative result or incomplete hypothesis. It does not require manufacturing an improving score.

## Before the clock starts

- Name the visible defect and exact reference locations.
- Freeze accepted inputs, registration, masks, foreground and scoring protocol.
- Identify reusable evidence and independently supported source content.
- Declare physical parameters, nuisance stages, evaluation caps and checkpoints.
- Record actual UTC start, stage deadlines and overall deadline.

Use the [experiment template](../../templates/experiment.md). Preserve the incumbent separately from experimental component states.

## Focused clear-play study: two hours

| Block | Maximum | Outcome |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Intake and controls | 10 min | Frozen G05 state, source/filter support and reproducible baseline. |
| Source blur before refraction | 50 min | Compare source-space and output-space softness at matched central detail. |
| Outline shading | 45 min | Test a justified surface-derived transport or illumination hypothesis. |
| Verification and decision | 15 min | Independent reproduction, local/overall guards and visible-feedback review. |

Skip dispersion for this reference. Freeze geometry during the blur comparison. Do not add spatially painted edge corrections or change a filter radius by image location.

Outline calculations must use the same optical surface. Unknown reflected illumination or unresolved internal paths remain limitations; neither permits target-colored repair.

## Subsequent element studies: four hours

| Stage | Maximum | Primary question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Refraction, including intake | 80 min | Does one realizable surface explain source-feature correspondence? |
| Blurriness | 20 min | Which declared material operator explains detail attenuation? |
| Color response | 20 min | Does a shared response improve informative references? |
| Outline | 60 min | Do surface transport and supported lighting explain the boundary? |
| Shadow / glow | 15 min | Does generated exterior behavior pass its original guards? |
| Icon shape / material | 20 min | Are foreground geometry and response independently supported? |
| Dispersion identification | 10 min | Is a spectral effect distinguishable from source color and filtering? |
| Final review | 15 min | What improved, failed, transferred or remains uncertain? |

The four-hour total governs this schedule. Skip unsupported or irrelevant stages with a recorded reason. Do not extend another block by silently borrowing unused time.

## Subpass limits

Declare a short wall-time and trial budget for each hypothesis before execution. Use at most three substantive proposals per question, including rejected proposals.

Stop a proposal at invalid paths, unsupported required source samples, exhausted evaluations or its deadline. Preserve partial evidence. A failed invariant is not a visible-model negative until its software cause is resolved.

Close a stage when its declared question is answered. Early completion is allowed; waiting does not create evidence. Record elapsed time and any overrun honestly.

## Verification

| Claim | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Geometric correctness | Consistent surfaces/normals, complete branches, boundary rules and numerical controls. |
| Source validity | Whole body, all original scored/guard samples, bilinear texels and finite filter support. |
| Visible progress | Exact defect crops, local/overall measurements and disclosed regressions. |
| Numerical nonregression | Original protocol plus retained precision caveats; no hidden epsilon waiver. |
| Transfer | Shared equations, meaningful family parameters and held-out tests where supported. |

A component improvement is not a full-model acceptance. An imperceptible aggregate reduction alone does not resolve visible feedback.

## Cache and compositor checks

- Retain coverage, premultiplied lens/clear contributions and the exact pre-foreground integrated image when later stages reuse samples.
- Record array dtypes and sample phase. Compare like representations; preserve failed zero controls rather than relaxing tolerances silently.
- Replay backing-dependent foreground responses through their original chain. A fixed-opacity approximation needs proof, not an assumption.
- Material-only scores cannot certify underlay or partial-coverage changes. Report completed-image checks on the original masks separately; never rewrite historical scores.

## Recording and rotation

Save concise work in progress, configurations, code revisions and evidence identities in Git. Commit each coherent subpass or checkpoint, push immediately and verify the remote tip.

Finish with one inspectable report: hypothesis, enlarged reference/baseline/candidate crops, results, regressions, uncertainty and implications for the shared system. Do not generate expensive final media during an unpromising search.

Rotate to another suitable element after the full cycle. Keep the accepted baseline when experimental branches fail. Do not reopen a stalled microscopic walk without new discriminating evidence.

Interaction and final animation export are separate work. They are not prerequisites for completing this research pass.
