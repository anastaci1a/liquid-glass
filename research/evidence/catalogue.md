# Historical research reports

The website generates this library from the evidence index. Reports retain their numerical results, acceptance limits and original scientific scope.

Administrative attribution and machine paths are normalized. Earlier Git revisions retain the exact original text. A report does not supersede [current findings](../current/state.md) unless its acceptance scope says so.
