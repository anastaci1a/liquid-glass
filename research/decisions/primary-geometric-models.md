# Geometric models are primary

Status: active. Geometric versions replace empirical image fits as the project’s primary models, including while visible defects remain.

## Selection and evidence

| Claim | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Primary model | The version shown first and developed next. |
| Verified calculation | A stated numerical, path, source or reproduction check passed. |
| Accepted improvement | The stated visible defect improves with disclosed guard results. |
| Historical image fit | An archived empirical comparison, retained for evidence. |

Primary selection does not require beating an older image fit. A failed visual comparison does not send development back to independent displacement fields or normal adjustments.

## Gallery

- Show the latest selected geometric version first, with its remaining defects.
- Put empirical reconstructions and their motion under explicitly historical sections.
- Show newer rejected experiments with their results; do not silently substitute them for the selected geometric state.
- Mark controls awaiting a geometric version as pending. Do not label their empirical renders geometric.

[Current model registry](../../reconstruction/current-models.json) identifies selected versions. The [reconstruction gallery](../../guide/reconstruction.md) is the visual entry point; linked studies retain measurements and failures.

## Preservation

Keep prior sources, recipes, scores, arrays and acceptance records. Their historical conclusions remain evidence; they do not determine which modeling approach is primary now.
