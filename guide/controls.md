# Control-family observations

Control families provide different geometric and material tests. Their shared appearance does not imply identical shape, thickness, scattering or illumination.

[Apple references](sources.md#choosing-and-arranging-controls) · [Physical models](geometric-optics.md) · [Comparisons](reconstruction.md)

## Choose the control

| Reference family | Discriminating evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Clear circular button | Edge reversal, compressed features and quiet central distortion. |
| Elongated toolbar | Cap/body continuity, longitudinal curvature and text deformation. |
| Frosted panel | Source-detail attenuation and transmission/reflection separation. |
| Active slider or switch | Track visibility, changing shape and directional color structure. |
| Grouped navigation | Transfer across backgrounds and foregrounds. |

Select references with independently supported source content. A smooth background may support tone measurements while leaving refraction weakly identified.

## One interaction, several visual states

Record the control's state, pose, apparent footprint and source frame. Resting and manipulated states may be distinct physical hypotheses; do not combine them as interchangeable samples.

Measure geometry and material changes separately. A state transition is not evidence for an arbitrary image warp.

## Sliders: keep the value visible

Apple's demonstration shows a more transparent, enlarged lens during manipulation. The colored track remains visible beneath it. Its resting white appearance is a separate observation.

Track endpoints, source reconstruction and directional filtering can be confounded. Keep these uncertainties explicit before assigning apparent color separation to dispersion.

## Switches and moving choice lenses

Use the same surface for intersection, normals, silhouette and section views. Test whether shape or material parameters transfer across selected positions and background colors.

A shared law may require physically meaningful family parameters. Independently fitted coefficients for every reference do not establish transfer.

## Buttons and their destinations

For clear buttons, compare source-feature correspondence through the center, reversal band and rim. Foreground vector shape and compositing remain separate from the refracted background.

For expanded surfaces, distinguish motion, thickness change and increased scattering. An illustrative animation alone does not validate dynamic optics.

## Review in motion

| Question | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Does the source move correctly? | Actual re-evaluated optical queries at each pose. |
| Does the material transfer? | Shared equations and declared family parameters. |
| Is the path valid? | Complete branches and finite source/filter support. |
| Is the rendering fast? | Separate device measurements. |

Static fidelity, temporal behavior and performance are independent claims.
